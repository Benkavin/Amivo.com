Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

amivo gives you a short message of encouragement each morning and a gentle question to reflect on each evening. It's built to keep what you write private. This page explains what amivo stores, where, and why. It covers the website at amivo.ca and the amivo apps for iPhone and Android.

What stays on your device

Your reflections (how you're feeling, what you're grateful for and anything else you write), your message style and your notification times are saved only on your phone or in your browser. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include them, just like other app data. If you delete the app or clear your browser data without a backup, they are gone.

Notifications in the apps

In the iPhone and Android apps, your phone schedules the morning and evening reminders itself. Nothing about your reminders is sent to us.

Notifications on the website

On the website, reminders are sent with web push. When you turn them on, we store the push address your browser gives us, your chosen times, your time zone and your message style, so we can send each reminder at the right moment. We don't receive your name, email or location from this. When you turn notifications off, or use “Clear all amivo data”, that record is deleted.

Daily messages

To show the day's message, amivo asks our server for today's date and your message style. Like most web servers, ours may briefly log basic request details such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure.

Who handles the data

Our server runs on Supabase, which stores the notification records described above. Push notifications are delivered through your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla). We don't sell your information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Children

amivo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can delete any reflection, or everything at once from Settings with “Clear all amivo data”. You can turn notifications off at any time in amivo or in your phone's settings. To ask a question or request deletion of any information we hold, email us at the address below.

Changes

If we change how amivo handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions? Email support@amivo.ca.
