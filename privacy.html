Privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

amivo gives you a short message of encouragement each morning and a gentle question to reflect on each evening. It's built to keep what you write private. This page explains what amivo stores, where, and why. It covers the website at amivo.ca and the amivo apps for iPhone and Android.

What stays on your device

Your reflections (how you're feeling, what you're grateful for and anything else you write), your message style and your notification times are saved only on your phone or in your browser. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include them, just like other app data. If you delete the app or clear your browser data without a backup, they are gone.

Notifications in the apps

In the iPhone and Android apps, your phone schedules the morning and evening reminders itself. Nothing about your reminders is sent to us.

Your account on the website

To get notifications on the website, you sign up with your email address, or with your Apple or Google account (we then receive only the email address they share with us). We use it only to sign you in (we email you a link or code, with no password) and to link your notifications to you. We don't send marketing emails. To delete your account, email us at the address below.

Notifications on the website

On the website, reminders are sent with web push. When you turn them on, we store the push address your browser gives us, your chosen times, your time zone and your message style, linked to your account, so we can send each reminder at the right moment. We don't receive your name or location from this. When you turn notifications off, sign out, or use “Clear all amivo data”, that record is deleted.

Premium and payments

amivo Premium is bought on the website through Stripe, our payment processor. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them. We keep only what we need to know you have Premium: your plan, whether it's active or in a free trial, its renewal or trial-end date, and Stripe's reference numbers for your account. Stripe handles your payment information under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy).

Ads on the free plan

On the website, the free plan shows ads from Google AdSense. Premium members never see ads, and the ads code isn't loaded for them. Ads never appear in the journal, and nothing you write is shared with Google or anyone else. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads and measure them, and may personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first. The iPhone and Android apps don't show ads.

Daily messages

To show the day's message, amivo asks our server for today's date and your message style. Like most web servers, ours may briefly log basic request details such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure.

Who handles the data

Our server runs on Supabase, which stores your account email, the notification records and the Premium status described above. Push notifications are delivered through your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla). Payments are handled by Stripe, and ads on the free plan by Google, as described above. We don't sell your information, we don't use your reflections or account for advertising, and we don't use analytics tools.

Children

amivo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can delete any reflection, or everything at once from Settings with “Clear all amivo data”. You can turn notifications off at any time in amivo or in your phone's settings. To ask a question or request deletion of any information we hold, email us at the address below.

Changes

If we change how amivo handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions? Email support@amivo.ca.
